## Current public form fields

The current Luminate Request a Demo form asks for:

- First Name *
- Last Name *
- Work Email *
- Phone *
- Company *
- Job Title *
- Country *
- Annual Revenue *
- Industry *
- "Do you want to register your music?" *
- Referral *
- optional marketing communications consent

For FANDEX, "Do you want to register your music?" must be answered **No**. FANDEX is requesting licensed access to Luminate data, not registering music or applying to become an Official Luminate Reporter.

Do not submit placeholder personal/company values. Complete the required identity/company fields only with the submitter's actual information.

Industry:
- choose the closest truthful option available in the live form
- do not select a category merely to improve sales routing

Annual Revenue:
- choose the truthful current range available in the live form

Referral:
- choose the truthful source by which Luminate was found

Marketing consent:
- optional; not required for the FANDEX licensing request

## Interest / free-text message

Use the following substance if the live form provides a free-text sales-interest field:

FANDEX is evaluating Luminate Music Data Share via Snowflake for a K-pop market-intelligence product. We need licensed album-level physical Product Sales data for an initial IU research path covering The Winning (2024-02-20) and Pieces (2021-12-29), initially in one US or CA territory lane.

We are looking for the subscription/package that provides Snowflake Music Data Share with album-level daily consumption detail, including Musical Release Group identity and physical Product Sales fields sufficient to work with REPORTED_QUANTITY, TRANSACTION_TYPE, REPORT_DATE, PRODUCT_FORMAT, COUNTRY_CODE and MODIFIED_AT.

Our planned public output is a FANDEX-derived metric only. We are not seeking to redistribute raw Luminate rows. Before onboarding, we need to confirm commercial product use, normalized observation storage/retention, public publication of the derived metric, post-termination handling, historical coverage for the 2021 and 2024 release windows, and the purchased-share object inventory.

Please connect us with the appropriate Music Data Share sales/licensing representative and, if possible, provide the relevant package/pricing structure and a technical contact for Snowflake Data Share scope validation.

- Snowflake credentials
- passwords
- API keys/tokens
- private keys
- database URLs
- provider payloads
- raw licensed quantities
- Neon connection details
- internal write-grant digests

## After submission

Retain only operational metadata:
- submission date
- channel = Luminate Request a Demo
- submitter identity used
- company identity used
- copy of the exact message submitted
- confirmation/reference ID if Luminate provides one
- response contact name/address when received

Do not mark authorization as resolved from a form submission or a sales reply alone. Authorization becomes eligible only after an executed order form or separate writing satisfies the existing FANDEX Luminate authorization gate.
